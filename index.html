Stop Examination Impersonation Before It Happens.

Traditional ID cards can be forged or swapped. Biometrics cannot. ImpedeX confirms that the candidate presenting for an examination is the registered student — using fingerprint matching at the checkpoint, every time, without exception.

  • Eliminates ghost students from examination halls.
  • Real-time alerts to invigilators upon mismatch.
  • Permanent audit trail of who entered which hall.

Registration Photo

A clear photo uploaded when the account is created travels with the student record. At the exam-day checkpoint it is displayed alongside the fingerprint match so the invigilator can confirm, at a glance, that the person in front of them is the candidate being seated.

Fingerprint Verification

High-resolution fingerprint scanning maps distinct ridge patterns for near-instantaneous identification — one live finger is matched against every enrolled template in the hall, giving a robust layer of identity proof that a physical card alone cannot offer.

Examination Conduct

Every candidate is expected to observe the following regulations set by the Federal University of Technology, Owerri during all examinations.

  • Present your student ID card and confirm your biometric identity at the examination hall entrance.
  • Arrive at least 30 minutes before the scheduled examination time.
  • Only permitted writing materials are allowed; phones and electronic devices are strictly prohibited.
  • Sit only at your allocated seat as assigned by the examination system.
  • No impersonation, collaboration, or any form of examination malpractice.
  • Remain seated and silent until the invigilator collects all scripts and grants permission to leave.

Exam-Day Verification

Verify student identity and allocate seats at checkpoint

Step 1 — Select Level

Choose the level whose exam is being written at this checkpoint (100 – 500).

Step 2 — Select Course —

Click the course the students are writing today.

Step 3 — Fingerprint Scan —

Place the student's finger on the connected exam scanner (ZK9500). On a match, the student's record, registration photo and allocated seat pop up automatically.

Put your finger on the fingerprint scanner for verification.
